# High-Value Feature Extraction (HVFE): The "Secret Sauce" Layer

**Document Version:** 2.0 (Deep Integration)
**Strategic Goal:** Extracting the "Value-Maximizers" from the Qwen UC Catalog (UC-001 to UC-110) to elevate the UDB from a tool to a high-end service.

---

## 1. The "Intelligent Planner" Suite (UC-044, UC-022, UC-015)
*   **The Feature:** **Weekly Planning Assistant (UC-044)**
*   **The Value:** Solves "Parental Cognitive Load." Instead of manual entry, the AI suggests a balanced week based on the child's **Skill Gap Analysis (UC-036)**.
*   **Technical Twist:** The AI checks for overlaps using the **Conflict Resolution Engine (UC-022)** and automatically schedules "Deep Work" blocks.

## 2. The "Mastery & Celebration" Engine (UC-031, UC-032, UC-037)
*   **The Feature:** **Goal-Activity Mapping (UC-032)**
*   **The Value:** Every activity is tied to a higher purpose. "Brushing teeth" isn't just a task; it's 2% progress toward the "Independence Milestone."
*   **The Feature:** **Goal Completion Celebration (UC-037)**
*   **The Value:** Real-world validation. Upon 100% goal completion, the system generates a **Custom PDF Certificate** and triggers a "Doter Evolution" event.

## 3. The "Evidence & Feedback" Loop (UC-019, UC-048, UC-059)
*   **The Feature:** **Activity Evidence Gallery (UC-019/UC-059)**
*   **The Value:** Creates a "Digital Scrapbook" of growth. Parents and children can leave **Activity Comments & Feedback (UC-048)**, turning the app into a shared journal of development.
*   **Technical Twist:** AI analyzes the evidence (photo/video) to provide "Pro-Tips" (e.g., "Great job on the drawing! Next time, try using more shading to create depth").

## 4. The "Live Coach" & Intervention Layer (UC-047, UC-036)
*   **The Feature:** **Skill Gap Analysis (UC-036)**
*   **The Value:** Proactive Parenting. The system identifies that a child hasn't practiced "Social Skills" in 10 days and notifies the parent with a "Recommended Social Quest."
*   **The Feature:** **Parent-Child Messaging (UC-047)**
*   **The Value:** The "Live Coach" AI can jump into the chat to facilitate a difficult conversation between parent and child using "Non-Violent Communication" (NVC) templates.

---

## 5. Final Build Order Refinement for Replit AI
We are moving these "Value-Maximizers" into the **Phase 1 Core**:
1.  **Core Architecture:** UUP + Goal-Activity Mapping.
2.  **The Planner:** Weekly Assistant + Conflict Engine.
3.  **The Feedback Loop:** Evidence Gallery + AI Feedback.
4.  **The Reward:** Celebration Engine + Transactional Ledger.
