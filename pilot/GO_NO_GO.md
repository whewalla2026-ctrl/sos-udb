# Pilot Go/No-Go Criteria
GO:
- Phase A: value shown to 10 users within 5 minutes onboarding; TTV < 2 min; WAU rising; AI hints engaged; cost within guardrails
- Phase B: onboarding complete, 30 users active, cost per user within target; no critical issues; Phase 2 ready artifacts exist
- Phase C: stable performance, retention rising, cost under threshold
NO-GO:
- Onboarding friction > 25% or TTV > 5 minutes
- AI hints fail to deliver usable guidance or cost out of control
- Vector fallback not reliable or tests fail
- Data migration risk unmitigated; data integrity risk observed
- System failures that impact planning or core UUP fields
