- If someone asks: why is this architecture overkill? Answer brutally: Because we tried to bake a multi-pillar, AI-driven system with real-time sync on a lean pilot; it becomes unmanageable at scale and expensive quickly. A focused academic core with a planner and hints is enough to validate product-market fit, costs, and user value; everything else should be staged after proven ROI.
- If a competitor beats us: small, clean MVP with repeatable onboarding and measurable value will win; they’ll avoid heavy AI orchestration and data-layer complexity in early stages.
- Two engineers: a lean monolith with bounded contexts (UUP core, LMS adapter, AI-lite hints, planner) and a minimal observability stack can deliver Phase 2 under 4 weeks instead of trying to build a full blown distributed system.
