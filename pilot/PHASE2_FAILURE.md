Phase 2 Lean Pilot — Failure Criteria

Data & Migration:
- Migration mapping fails or rollback cannot restore previous state without data loss

AI & Cost:
- AI latency > 500 ms consistently; fallback not engaged; cost exceeds guardrails

Monitoring:
- No observability; cannot detect issues quickly

LMS:
- Canvas adapter broken, onboarding fully blocked

UX:
- onboarding friction > 25% or drop-off > 50%

If any of the above, STOP Phase 2 and replan 1–2 sprints to fix blockers.
