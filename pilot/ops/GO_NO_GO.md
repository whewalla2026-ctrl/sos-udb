# Pilot Go/No-Go — Phase 2 Lean (10 → 30 → 100)

GO criteria (Phase 2):
- Phase 2 KPIs met: TTV, WAU growth, AI hints usage, planner engagement, error rate within targets
- Phase 2 costs within guardrails; AI budget under cap; vector usage under budget
- Phase 2 gating artifacts attached; Phase 2 demo complete and approved by governance

NO-GO criteria:
- Onboarding friction ≥ 25%, TTV > 5 minutes for onboarding
- AI hints failing: > 25% timeouts or poor usefulness signals
- Budget or vector usage exceeding guardrails; costs out of line with projections
- System reliability: critical outages or data integrity issues during pilot

Escalation path:
- If NO-GO, halt progression and fix blockers; re-run Phase 2 gate with updated evidence.
