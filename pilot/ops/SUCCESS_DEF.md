# Pilot Success Definition (LEAN Phase 2)

10 users: Phase 2 target minimal viable, stable operations, and demonstrable value.
- Value: 1-2 tangible outcomes per user per week (planning benefits and hints that aid study).
- Reliability: low error rate, stable latency, no data-loss risk.
- Economics: AI hints kept under budget; vector usage controlled; costs within guardrails.
- Onboarding: fast, intuitive, under 2 minutes.

30 users: Phase 2 demonstrates scale, stable ops, and user retention signals.

100 users: Real validation; cost remains predictable; on-boarding and value delivery are proven at scale.
