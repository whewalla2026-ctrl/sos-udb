# UDB — Unified Developmental Backbone
### The AI-Powered Platform for Child Development

---

## What is UDB?

UDB is the first platform that unifies **academic achievement, physical wellness, social development, and financial literacy** into a single AI-driven system for children ages 6-23 — with full parental oversight and institutional compliance.

## The Problem

Parents juggle 5-7 apps to track their child's homework, sleep, screen time, savings, and social life. Schools have no unified view of student wellbeing. Neither has AI that adapts to each child.

## The Solution

| Pillar | What UDB Does | How AI Helps |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Academic** | Quest-based learning with evidence submission | Socratic AI tutor that guides without giving answers |
| **Wellness** | Sleep, activity, and focus tracking | Proactive alerts when biometric patterns indicate stress |
| **Social** | Study pods, collaborative quests, messaging | Content moderation + safety scoring |
| **Financial** | Escrow marketplace for kid-preneurs | Age-gated limits ($200/<16, $500/16-18) with parent MFA |

## Key Differentiators

- **Doter Companion**: A virtual pet that reflects real-world health and habits — gamification that drives behavior change
- **COPPA + GDPR Compliant**: Credit card micro-charge verification, right-to-be-forgotten, audit log immutability
- **Safety Score**: Composite 0-100 wellness indicator with automatic parent alerts
- **Soulbound Tokens**: Blockchain-verified achievement credentials that follow the child
- **Weekly AI Planner**: Generates personalized schedules based on chronotype, workload, and wellness data

## Technical Highlights

- 18-container production stack (NestJS API, Next.js frontend, PostgreSQL, Redis, full observability)
- 535+ automated tests, 100% pass rate
- Argon2id password hashing, JWT auth, rate limiting, non-root containers
- 13 feature flags for safe, gradual rollout
- Multi-tenant ready for institutional deployment
